Medical visits in English or Spanish in the Roma and Condesa neighborhoods: hotel visits from MXN $2,500 (Zone A, standard hours), house calls from MXN $1,300, and a clinic in Roma by appointment for MXN $1,200. Estimated arrival: about 45 minutes, depending on traffic; it may be shorter.

Fees in Roma and Condesa

Both neighborhoods are in hotel visit Zone A and share the same house call fee. The clinic is in Roma; there is no Condesa location. Amounts in Mexican pesos, per visit:

Service Standard After-hours Holiday
Hotel visit$2,500$4,000$5,500
House call$1,300$3,000$4,000
Roma clinic (by appointment)$1,200—$2,500

The fee depends on the time and day, and is confirmed when you request the visit; holidays are arranged via WhatsApp only. Standard, after-hours and holiday times are in the fees section, with the same fees.

Deposit and cancellation. Hotel visits need no deposit: they are paid directly to the doctor, in Mexican pesos. House calls are requested via WhatsApp and confirmed with a bank transfer of $650, 50% of the standard fee. The rest, and the difference for after-hours or holiday visits, is paid to the doctor at the visit. If you cancel at least 3 hours in advance, the deposit is refunded; with less than 3 hours' notice, or if you are not at the address at the agreed time, it is non-refundable.

If you are staying in a short-term rental

When you message on WhatsApp, say whether you are at a hotel, a short-term rental apartment or a private home, and give the address. That way the applicable fee is confirmed before the visit. Have the building name and apartment number ready and, if there is a security desk, let them know you are expecting the doctor.

How long it takes to arrive

Subject to availability, the estimated arrival time in Roma and Condesa is about 45 minutes, depending on traffic; it may be shorter. When you book, send a WhatsApp to +52 595 109 3969 and you will be given an estimated time.

Clinic in Roma

By appointment on weekday evenings and Saturday mornings. The clinic is at Briyam Coworking Médico, Roma Sur location: Monterrey 243, piso 5, Roma Sur, Cuauhtémoc, CDMX. Your appointment is confirmed once you pay the 50% deposit online by card ($600 of $1,200); the rest is paid at the clinic. Detailed hours and cancellation policy are in the fees section.
