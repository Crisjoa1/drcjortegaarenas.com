A doctor at your hotel: information for concierges

How to request a bilingual (English/Spanish) medical visit for a guest, what it costs by zone and time of day, and which documents the guest receives for travel insurance. Outpatient general medicine.

WhatsApp +52 595 109 3969 Book a hotel visit
This is not an emergency service. If the guest has chest pain, trouble breathing, loss of consciousness, heavy bleeding, signs of a stroke or any other emergency, call 911 or take them to a hospital emergency room.

Step by step

How to request the doctor

01

Message or book

Via WhatsApp at +52 595 109 3969, or online at cal.com/drcjortegaarenas/visita-en-hotel. Either the concierge or the guest can request it.

02

Send these details

Hotel name and address, room number, guest name, preferred language and a contact phone number. There is no need to send medical details by message: they are discussed during the visit.

03

Confirmation and arrival

Availability and the applicable fee (by zone, time and day) are confirmed. Estimated arrival time: 30–45 minutes, depending on traffic.

04

Visit and payment

The visit takes place in the room and is paid directly to the doctor in Mexican pesos: card (portable terminal), bank transfer or cash. For a first visit, the guest can fill in the forms beforehand at drcjortegaarenas.com/formularios.

Travel insurance

Documents the guest receives

Useful for an insurance reimbursement claim. Whether a claim is accepted depends on each guest's policy.

  • Written prescription, when treatment is indicated.
  • Official tax invoice (CFDI) or insurance reimbursement receipt, only for card payments. No tax receipts are issued for bank transfers or cash.
  • English-language Travel Clinical Summary, on request for an additional fee: $500 digital (PDF by email) or $800 printed (delivered after the visit).
