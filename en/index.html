Booking

How to book

Book online in four steps. Your appointment is securely confirmed and all details are sent to your email.

01

Choose your visit

Book online the type of care you need: teleconsultation, in-office (Del Valle, Roma, Polanco or Santa Fe), home or hotel visit.

02

Confirm with your deposit

Teleconsultation and in-office visits are confirmed upon online card payment (50% deposit for in-office; full fee for teleconsultation). Home and hotel visits are paid directly with the physician, and their cost depends on zone, schedule and day.

03

Receive your confirmation

You'll get an email with your appointment details and a reminder the day before. For in-office visits, the exact address is shared privately only upon confirmation, for security.

04

Complete your forms

First visit only: informed consent and health questionnaire at drcjortegaarenas.com/formularios. Follow-up visits do not require them.

FAQ

Common questions

How do I book?

Online at cal.com/drcjortegaarenas or via WhatsApp at +52 595 109 3969.

Do you speak English?

Yes. Bilingual care in Spanish and English for local and international patients.

Where are the clinics?

Del Valle, Roma, Polanco and Santa Fe. The exact address is shared privately upon confirmation.

Do you handle emergencies?

No. Services are strictly outpatient general medicine. In an emergency, go immediately to a hospital ER.

How long does it take to reach my hotel?

Approximately 30–45 minutes, depending on the area and traffic. After booking, send a WhatsApp to +52 595 109 3969 to confirm.

Will I get documents for my travel insurance?

When treatment is indicated, a written prescription is provided for your claim; acceptance depends on your policy. If you pay by card, you can request an official invoice (CFDI) or a receipt for reimbursement. The English-language Travel Clinical Summary has an additional fee ($500 digital, $800 printed).

What are the clinic hours?

Monday to Friday 5–8 pm and Saturdays 11 am–3 pm, by appointment. Telemedicine, hotel visits and house calls have their own hours (see Services).
